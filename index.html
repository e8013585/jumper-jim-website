What We Do

Mobile 12V Jump Starts

Battery too weak to start your vehicle? Jumper Jim comes to where your vehicle is with a professional portable jump starter. We connect it to your vehicle's 12-volt battery and give your engine the boost it needs to try again. No second car, no jumper-cable hunt.

  • We come to your location
  • Cars, SUVs and pickup trucks
  • Gas, diesel and hybrid with compatible 12V systems
  • Upfront price before dispatch

Good to know: A jump start gives a discharged battery a boost. It doesn't repair or replace the battery, and some vehicles need more than a jump to get going.
